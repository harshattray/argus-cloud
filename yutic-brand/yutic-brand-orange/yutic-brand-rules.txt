YUTIC — BRAND RULES v1.0 — ORANGE SCHEME

MARK  A fan of five peacock feathers, each with an eye. Never rotated, reordered,
      stretched, recoloured or given effects. Clearspace = one eye diameter.
      Minimum 28px wide; below that drop the quill and base.

LOCKUP  Fan left, wordmark 'Yutic' beside it, capital Y. Stacked only where width is constrained.

COLOUR  Feather orange #E8641C is the only chromatic colour. Shaft black #16130F, Pupil peach #F9CFAF, Paper #EFEDE9.
        Greys: #FCFBF9 #E4DFD7 #BDB5AA #8A8177 #554E45 #2C2620.
        The accent stays under ~10% of a surface. Never body copy on light grounds.
        Orange and plum are both official. One scheme per surface, never mixed.

TYPE  DM Mono for wordmark, headlines, labels, numbers. DM Sans for body text.
      Display tracking -0.04em; body 15-17px at line-height 1.7.

VOICE  Plain and factual. Say what it does and who it is for. No mission language.

PRODUCTS  Yutic is the quiet parent. Products keep their own identity.
          Endorsement appears once per surface, in the footer, never in a product header.
          It appears on app UI as well as marketing surfaces (changed 2026-08-20 by
          Harsha; previously read 'never in product headers or app UI'). Note this
          means it also reaches anyone holding a Normascope share link.
          Products are numbered in ship order: 01 Normascope.
