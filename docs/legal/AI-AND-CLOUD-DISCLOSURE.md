# AI and Normascope Cloud Disclosure

**Status:** waitlist-stage disclosure  
**Last updated:** 13 August 2026

Normascope Cloud is a planned hosted product. The waitlist does not provide
Cloud access and does not send screenshots, source code, DOM content, or AI
prompts to a hosted service.

## AI explanations are guidance

When hosted AI is introduced, an explanation may identify a possible cause of a
visual difference or suggest a possible repair. It may be inaccurate or
incomplete. It is guidance for a human to review, edit, ignore, or discard.

Normascope, Yutic, and the AI provider do not approve, guarantee, or take
responsibility for a code change, design decision, release decision, or other
outcome made from relying on an AI response, subject to applicable law.

The deterministic visual comparison, score, and CI pass/fail result remain
separate from AI. AI must not silently change them or apply a fix.

## Future hosted data flow

Before hosted uploads or hosted AI are enabled, we will present a clear
first-use disclosure describing what data leaves the user's machine, why it is
used, which providers process it, retention, deletion, available exclusions,
and whether a report can be hosted without hosted AI.

The paid Cloud Terms, Privacy Policy, Subprocessors Notice, and refund policy
will be published before paid Cloud checkout is enabled.

Questions may be sent to **waitlist@normascope.com**.
