## Current website use

The public Normascope website is designed to work without advertising cookies
or cross-site tracking cookies. We do not use cookies to sell advertising or
to build an advertising profile about visitors.

The site may use strictly necessary browser storage or cookies for security,
session, rate-limit, or access-gate functions. These are used to provide a
requested feature or protect the site and cannot be disabled through a
marketing preference panel without affecting that feature.

The waitlist form may record technical abuse-prevention signals, such as a
request timestamp or rate-limit state. This is not used for advertising.

## Audience measurement

We use **Vercel Web Analytics**, provided by Vercel Inc., to count visits to
the public pages of normascope.com. We use it to understand how many people
reach the site and which pages lead to a waitlist signup.

This tool does not use cookies and does not store or read anything on your
device. It is served from our own domain, so no third-party host receives a
request from these pages.

For each page view it records the page address, the referring site, any
campaign parameters in the link you followed, and general technical details
such as browser, operating system, device type, and country. It does not
receive your name or email address.

Visitors are counted using a value calculated from technical request details.
That value is recalculated each day, so the same person visiting on two
different days is counted as two visitors. We cannot use it to identify you,
to follow you across other websites, or to link a page view to a waitlist
signup.

Vercel Web Analytics does not run on the password-protected areas of the site
or on shared report pages.

## Future analytics or marketing tools

If we add advertising, cross-site tracking, or any other non-essential tool
that stores or reads information on your device, we will update this Notice
and provide the consent or opt-out controls required by applicable law before
activating it.

## Your choices

You can control cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Some
strictly necessary functions may stop working if those controls are disabled.
