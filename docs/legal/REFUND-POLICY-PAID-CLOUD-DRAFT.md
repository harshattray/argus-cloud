# Normascope Cloud Refund Policy

**Status:** draft for paid Cloud launch — not currently active  
**Last updated:** 13 August 2026

Do not publish this as an active policy or link it from a paid checkout until
the final refund terms have been reviewed and the Paddle checkout flow is
configured. Normascope Cloud is not currently available for purchase through
the waitlist.

## Planned launch policy

The planned launch offer includes a 30-day money-back guarantee for the
Normascope Cloud subscription, subject to final exclusions and applicable
consumer-protection law. The final version must explain:

- how a customer requests a refund;
- whether the guarantee applies to the subscription, credit packs, or both;
- how refunds interact with consumed credits and hosted work;
- cancellation timing and access after cancellation;
- statutory rights that cannot be waived; and
- the support route and expected processing timeline.

Paddle's then-current buyer terms and refund policy will also apply to the
payment transaction where applicable. If our final policy differs from
Paddle's standard refund policy, the checkout must link to our policy.

## Finalization gate

Before paid checkout is enabled, replace this draft with final wording reviewed
for the seller's jurisdiction and confirm it against the live Paddle catalog,
checkout, receipts, cancellation flow, and webhook/refund handling.

Contact for policy questions: **waitlist@normascope.com**.
