## ~~Post-intro supplement~~ — withdrawn 2026-08-19

This section recomputed the same recorded usage at $3/$15 per MTok, the list
price Sonnet 5 was scheduled to rise to on 2026-09-01, and produced the
**$0.0164 per review** figure quoted throughout `FUTURENORMA.md`.

**That increase is not happening.** Anthropic's pricing page now states that the
$2/$10 introductory price "is now the standard price" and the rise to $3/$15
"will not occur". So there is no post-intro case to survive: the $0.0115
measured above was always the real number, and $0.0164 was a forecast of an
event that was cancelled.

Consequences, all in the safe direction:

- `usage.ts` carried $3/$15 for Sonnet, so **every recorded cost for it was 50%
  high**. Corrected 2026-08-19. Recorded spend was over-stated, never
  under-stated, so no budget or breaker was ever too loose.
- Credits are derived from cost, so the correction moved a sonnet analysis from
  5 credits to 4 — see the hosted section.
- Pack prices were seeded against the higher figure, so they now clear their 3×
  floor by more than intended, not less.

Nothing had ever checked the price table against the page. `calibrate-hosted.mjs`
now refuses to run while the two disagree, which is how this was found.

**Crop grounding is 2.3× cheaper, not more expensive.** G4 was written on the
assumption that crops raise COGS. They do add ~600 input tokens — and they cut
output from ~1,700 tokens to ~519, because a model that can see the difference
stops writing paragraphs about what it cannot determine. Output is 5× the price
of input, so the trade is heavily in our favour.

Target ≤ $0.08 per review: **met, ~10× over**, on the crop-grounded figure.

### What this does not cover

- **Nine calls on four fixtures.** Enough to establish the crop-vs-metadata
  direction, which is large and consistent; not enough to be a forecast. The CLI
  calibration above used 22.
- **No batch measurement on the hosted path.** The 50% batch rate is applied
  arithmetically by `computeCostMicrodollars`; it has been measured on the CLI
  path (Phase B, B3) and not on this one.
- **No prompt-cache mix at scale.** Two calls here wrote the system-prompt cache
  and the rest did not, which is the cold-start shape rather than steady state.
  Real traffic will read the cache more often and cost less than these figures.
- **The Bose frame's crops were truncated.** It had 4 flagged regions; one
  build/reference pair at 1.24M pixels consumed 83% of the budget and the other
  three pairs were dropped. That is the budget working as designed, but it means
  a full-page capture grounds on one region while a short page grounds on four.
